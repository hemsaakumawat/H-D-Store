Privacy Policy

Learn how we collect, protect, and respect your personal information.

Last Updated: October 2026

Welcome to H&D Gift Shop. Your privacy and trust are extremely important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website, place a gift inquiry, or contact us, and how we keep that information safe.

1. Information We Collect

When you use our website or submit our contact form, we may collect the following basic details:

  • Personal Details: Your full name and email address.
  • Order & Inquiries: The gift occasion (Birthday, Anniversary, etc.) and your custom gift requirements or message.
  • Delivery Details: Shipping address and contact phone number (when placing an order).

2. How We Use Your Information

We only use your personal details for genuine gifting services:

  • To process, pack, and deliver your gift orders accurately.
  • To answer your questions, messages, and custom order inquiries.
  • To customize greeting cards, names, and initials on personalized gift items.
  • To notify you about delivery updates or order confirmations.

3. Protection of Your Information

We take your privacy seriously. We implement reasonable physical and digital security measures to protect your data. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third parties or advertisers.

4. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to social media or payment services. Please note that we are not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites. We encourage you to read their privacy policies separately.

5. Your Rights

You have the full right to ask what details we have stored about your order, or request us to delete your contact inquiry data at any time by contacting our support team.
